MoneyPlanney

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

MoneyPlanney works completely offline. It has no account, no server and no tracking. Your budget stays on your phone, and it leaves only when you export it yourself.

What we collect

Nothing. MoneyPlanney does not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. We have no servers that the app talks to, so there is nothing for us to receive.

What the app does not use

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • No network requests. The app does not send or fetch data over the internet.
  • No iCloud sync. Your data is not copied to any cloud by the app.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter (your payday, income, envelopes, bills, goals, debts and expenses) is stored in a database on your phone. The database is encrypted, and its key is kept in the iOS Keychain on this device only.

App lock and Face ID

If you turn on app lock, your phone checks your face or passcode. MoneyPlanney only learns whether it matched. It never sees or stores your face data.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are created and shown by your phone. They never leave the device.

Backups and exports

A backup file or spreadsheet export is created only when you ask for it, and you choose where it goes (Files, AirDrop, email or another app). Backups are password-protected by default. We cannot open them and cannot recover a forgotten password. Once a file leaves the app, it is under the control of the place you sent it.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data erases everything the app holds on your phone. Deleting the app removes it as well. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected.

Links that open in your browser

The Privacy policy and Support links in Settings open this website in your phone's browser. The app itself makes no network request; your browser does, and the website host (GitHub Pages) may log your visit under its own policy.

Children

MoneyPlanney collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
